About Us

Galacon is a trusted infrastructure company delivering civil, industrial and commercial projects with precision. We combine engineering excellence with innovation, safety and on-time execution.

Our Story

Galacon Infrastructure and Projects Pvt Ltd was founded in 2006 with a single conviction—that infrastructure built with integrity outlasts everything else. What began as a focused civil engineering firm has grown into one of India's trusted names in large-scale infrastructure delivery, with a presence across 13 states and a portfolio spanning refineries, highways, substations, data centres, and more.

Over the years, we have earned the trust of some of India's most demanding clients—ISRO, NTPC, Indian Oil, Reliance, and MRPL among them. Their confidence in us is a reflection of our unwavering commitment to quality, safety, and accountability on every project we undertake.

Mission Statement

At Galacon our unwavering commitment is to build a future where excellence in construction meets unparalleled success. With a steadfast vision, we aim to grow into a 500 crore turnover company within the next 8 years, leaving an indelible mark on the construction industry.

Excellence in Craftsmanship: We pledge to deliver projects of superior quality, ensuring that each structure we create stands as a testament to our dedication to precision, innovation, and lasting value.

Customer-Centric Approach: Our clients are the cornerstone of our success. We will consistently listen to their needs, collaborate closely, and exceed expectations to create long-lasting partnerships built on trust and satisfaction.

Innovation and Sustainability: Embracing cutting-edge technologies and sustainable practices, we aspire to lead the industry in adopting eco-friendly solutions that minimize our environmental impact while maximizing the value we bring to society.

Empowering Our Team: Our success lies in the hands of our exceptional team members. By fostering a culture of learning, growth, and inclusivity, we aim to empower them to reach their full potential and drive the company's progress.

Safety First: The well-being of our employees, clients, and communities remains paramount. We are committed to maintaining the highest safety standards, creating secure work environments, and promoting health and well-being for all stakeholders.

Community Impact: As responsible corporate citizens, we will actively participate in community development initiatives, fostering positive change, and contributing to the betterment of society.

Integrity and Ethics: Our foundation is built on integrity and ethical conduct. We will conduct business with transparency, honesty, and fairness, ensuring the trust of our stakeholders remains unwavering.

Growth and Profitability: To achieve our ambitious goal of becoming a 500 crore turnover company, we will pursue strategic growth opportunities, optimize our operations, and drive profitability while upholding our values.
